## Participant summary (verbatim)

> "The "Define a ritual" sheet is **nicely forgiving because it lets me leave optional details alone** and still set a realistic weekly target. **I'd want a clearer creation acknowledgment in Today and a direct "Logged to: Morning walk" style cue in chat, because those are the two moments where confidence dipped.** The sparkle button is easy to spot, but **a tiny label like Chat or Log with AI** would make the route even more obvious."

## Issues analysis (verbatim)

> "The app's tone is genuinely reassuring — "momentum, not streaks" is exactly the kind of framing that makes habit tracking feel less punishing — but a few small feedback gaps made me second-guess what had actually happened. The "Try the demo" button was visible, though it was quieter [so I] had to pause and make sure I was choosing the path into the app rather than signup. Once inside, the Today dashboard was nicely uncluttered, but the "3/7" and "2 of 4 logged" labels took a moment to parse, so I was doing a tiny bit of mental math before I felt oriented. The biggest trust wobble came after creating "Morning walk": the sheet closed, but the new ritual wasn't visible on the dashboard, which made the save feel less certain even if the setup itself was simple. The AI chat is the strongest part of the flow — it's easy to open, the input sits exactly where you expect, and sending a sentence like a text message feels much more natural than hunting for a manual log button. At the end, the sent message bubble and typing dots made it clear the chat had my input, but there still wasn't confirmation that it had tied the message back to Morning walk yet, so waiting was the only sensible next step. Overall, the concept is strong and the demo is easy to get into, but ritual creation an[d loggi]ng need clearer acknowledgment so users don't keep wondering whether they're on the right track."
